🛡️ Guardian

Read the love. Skip the hate.

A free, open-source comment shield for creators. It hides bullying on YouTube, Instagram and X around the clock, blurs it on TikTok, and puts your kind comments first.

Try it right here

Runs entirely in your browser with Guardian's built-in word filter. Nothing is uploaded. The full app can use Claude, which also understands sarcasm.

Install in one command

# with uv (nothing else to install first)
uvx --from git+https://github.com/swetha-8219/Guardian guardian

# or with pip
pip install git+https://github.com/swetha-8219/Guardian
guardian

Guardian opens in your browser at localhost:5000. No keys needed to try it. Connect YouTube, Instagram or X when you want always-on protection.

What Guardian does

⏱️ Always-on protection

Checks YouTube every 2 min, X every 3, Instagram every 5, and hides bullying on the platform itself. One click to unhide a mistake.

💚 Kind comments first

A highlights feed of supportive comments. Honest feedback stays visible, because criticism isn't bullying.

🧩 Browser extension

Blurs hurtful comments as you scroll YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and X.

🚨 Threat alerts & evidence

Flags threats and repeat offenders, and exports a CSV so you can report without rereading.

💌 Weekly kindness email

A summary of the good parts of your week. The hate is never in it.

🤖 Works with Claude (MCP)

Ask Claude "how did people react to my latest video?" and get the love and the feedback, not the abuse.
